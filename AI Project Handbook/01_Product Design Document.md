**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Product Vision

This website is not intended to function merely as an online resume or portfolio.

It is designed to become a digital experience that introduces visitors to my professional journey through storytelling, interaction, and thoughtful design.

Instead of asking visitors to read about me, the website should allow them to experience who I am.

Every section should naturally guide visitors toward understanding my values, expertise, and the impact I have delivered throughout my career.

The website itself should become one of my strongest portfolio projects.

---

# 2. Product Objectives

The website should achieve the following goals:

* Introduce myself within the first few seconds.
* Demonstrate professionalism through design quality.
* Showcase both technical and business capabilities.
* Build trust through measurable achievements.
* Encourage recruiters to download my CV.
* Encourage hiring managers to explore my projects.
* Leave a memorable impression.

---

# 3. Primary Goal

The visitor should leave the website thinking:

> "This person doesn't just build software. He understands business problems, improves processes, and delivers measurable impact."

---

# 4. Target Audience

## Primary Persona

### Recruiters

Needs

* Quickly understand who I am.
* Download CV.
* Verify experience.
* See whether I fit the role.

Pain Points

* Limited time.
* Reviews many candidates daily.
* Doesn't enjoy reading long paragraphs.

---

## Secondary Persona

### Hiring Managers

Needs

* Evaluate project quality.
* Understand business impact.
* Review technical capabilities.
* Assess communication quality.

Pain Points

* Wants evidence, not buzzwords.

---

## Third Persona

### Technical Interviewers

Needs

* Understand implementation quality.
* Review architecture thinking.
* Evaluate engineering mindset.

Pain Points

* Wants depth rather than flashy visuals.

---

# 5. Positioning

The website should position me as someone operating between three disciplines:

* Business
* Engineering
* Technology

Instead of appearing as a developer who happens to know business, or an engineer who happens to know software, the website should communicate that I connect all three disciplines together.

# 11. Hero Section

## Goal

Immediately communicate who I am.

The visitor should understand my professional identity within five seconds.

### Content

Name

Professional Titles

Short Tagline

Scroll Indicator

Primary CTA

### Visual Direction

Large typography

Minimal background

Subtle motion

Lots of whitespace

No clutter

### Success Criteria

The visitor understands:

* My name
* My profession
* My positioning

Without reading a paragraph.

# 12. About Section

High readability.

# 15. Business Impact

Goal

Replace generic claims with measurable achievements.

Visitors trust numbers more than adjectives.

Examples:

Production Waste Reduction

Product Return Reduction

Workflow Automation

Operational Dashboard Development

Cost Optimization

Every achievement should include context.

Not just percentages.

Explain why it mattered.

# 20. UX Principles

The experience should prioritize:

Clarity over decoration.

Whitespace over clutter.

Quality over quantity.

Storytelling over listing.

Meaning over animation.

Consistency over novelty.

Every design decision should support readability.

---

# 21. Content Principles

Avoid long paragraphs.

Prefer:

Headlines

Short descriptions

Bullet points

Metrics

Visual hierarchy

Visitors should be able to understand each section within a few seconds.

---

# 22. Responsive Strategy

Desktop

Primary experience.

Tablet

Preserve storytelling.

Mobile

Maintain narrative while simplifying animations.

Content hierarchy must remain identical across devices.

---

# 23. Accessibility Goals

Keyboard navigation

Semantic HTML

Reduced motion support

Proper contrast

Descriptive links

Screen reader compatibility

Accessibility should be treated as part of the design rather than an afterthought.

---

# 24. Success Metrics

The website is considered successful if visitors can:

Understand who I am within 10 seconds.

Understand my expertise within 30 seconds.

Understand my value within 60 seconds.

Find my best projects within 90 seconds.

Download my CV within two minutes.

Contact me without confusion.

---

# 25. Product Principles

This website should never feel like:

A template.

A PowerPoint presentation.

A dashboard.

A résumé converted into HTML.

Instead, it should feel like a carefully crafted digital product.

Every interaction should reinforce trust.

Every animation should have a purpose.

Every section should move the visitor one step closer toward understanding my professional value.

When visitors finish scrolling, they should not simply remember the technologies I use.

They should remember the problems I solve.
