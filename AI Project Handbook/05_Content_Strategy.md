# Content Strategy

## Portfolio Website — Haris A. Majid

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Content Philosophy

The website should not feel like reading a résumé.

Instead, it should feel like discovering a person.

Every section should answer a question naturally.

Instead of saying:

"Here are my skills."

The website should answer:

"What kind of problems does Haris solve?"

---

# 2. Tone of Voice

The overall tone should be:

Professional

Confident

Calm

Thoughtful

Modern

Approachable

Avoid sounding:

Arrogant

Salesy

Corporate

Buzzword-heavy

Overly technical

Every sentence should be simple and meaningful.

---

# 3. Story Structure

The story follows one narrative.

WHO AM I

↓

WHERE DID I START

↓

WHAT DO I DO

↓

HOW DO I THINK

↓

WHAT HAVE I BUILT

↓

WHAT IMPACT HAVE I CREATED

↓

LET'S WORK TOGETHER

The visitor should never feel lost.

# 18. Writing Principles

Write for humans.

Not for search engines.

Not for ATS.

Not for developers.

Every sentence should answer one question.

Every paragraph should communicate one idea.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Use active voice.

Keep paragraphs short.

Prioritize clarity over clever wording.

---

# 19. SEO Writing Guidelines

Every section should naturally include relevant concepts such as:

Business Analysis

Digital Transformation

Business Process Improvement

Workflow Automation

Dashboard Development

Data Analytics

Power Platform

R&D Process Development

Manufacturing

Continuous Improvement

Do not force keywords.

Write naturally.

---

# 20. Final Content Principle

Visitors are not hiring me because I know Astro, React, or Power Platform.

They are hiring me because I understand problems, communicate clearly, and deliver solutions that create measurable impact.

The website should reinforce this idea from the first scroll to the last.

Technology is part of the story.

Business value is the main story.
